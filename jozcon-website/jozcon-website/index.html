About JOZCON

JOZCON COMPANY is a Randburg-based construction and renovation contractor serving homeowners, property developers and businesses across Johannesburg. We combine practical trade skills with proper project management, so clients get quality work, clear communication and a site that runs to plan.

Our mission

To deliver durable, well-finished construction work on time and on budget, with honest pricing and respect for every client’s property.

Our vision

To be Johannesburg’s most trusted name for residential and commercial building, known for workmanship that lasts.

Quality and safety

Every site follows OHS Act requirements, with PPE, toolbox talks and quality checks at each stage before we move on.

  • Registered South African company
  • Insured workmanship
  • Workmanship guarantee on completed jobs
